# Anniversary Flipbook

Open `index.html` to view the 10 original PDF pages on a blank white background. The flipbook is the only visible item.

- Click the right side of the page or press the right arrow to turn forward.
- Click the left side or press the left arrow to turn backward.
- Swipe horizontally on touchscreens.

The original pages are preserved in `assets/` as WebP images. Upload the entire folder to any static web host.
